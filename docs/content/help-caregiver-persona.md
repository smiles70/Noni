# Caregiver help — common questions

This page is for family members and friends who are buying or managing a mynaani gift for someone they care about.

---

## What is mynaani?

Mynaani is a calm, self-paced program that helps adults learn to use Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. The lessons are short. They use plain language. There are no tests, no timers, and no grades.

Mynaani is designed for adults who prefer to learn without pressure. The curriculum was built around geragogy, the study of how older adults learn best.

---

## What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant. You can think of it as a computer program that reads and writes in plain language. It can help with everyday tasks such as organizing a grocery list, writing a letter, or thinking through a decision.

Claude does not keep a memory of past conversations. Each time you start, it begins fresh. That is a design choice. It protects privacy and keeps things simple.

---

## What is geragogy?

Geragogy is the study of how adults learn in later life. It is different from teaching children or younger adults. Older adults bring a lifetime of experience. The best teaching protects confidence, reduces mental effort, and does not rush.

Mynaani applies this research to every part of the program. The screens are calm. The text is large and clear. The lessons move at the learner's pace. Nothing pops up, flashes, or hurries the learner.

---

## How is the curriculum organized?

The curriculum is made of modules. Each module covers one part of learning to use Claude.

- **Modules 0 and 1** are free. Anyone with an account can read them.
- **Modules 2, 3, 4, and 5** are available after a one-time purchase.

When you finish Module 1, you will see a page that explains how to continue. You can buy access for yourself or receive access as a gift.

Each module is made of units. Each unit contains four short page types:

- **Recap** — a brief reminder of what came before.
- **Principle** — the main idea of the unit, in plain language.
- **Example** — a familiar situation that shows the idea in action.
- **Retrieval** — a question with two choices to check understanding. There is no time limit.

---

## What are Claude Skills?

A Skill is a saved way of asking Claude to help. It is like a recipe the learner writes once and uses again. For example, a Skill might help Claude write a short letter to a family member.

Skills are covered in Module 4.

---

## What are Agents?

An Agent is a group of Skills working together on a larger task. The learner can build their own Agents in Module 5. An Agent might gather ideas, write a draft, and then read it back for review.

## What is a gift token?

A gift token is a short code that gives someone access to Modules 2, 3, 4, and 5. The recipient enters the token on the gift redemption page and access is added to their account.

Each token can be used once. Keep the token private until you give it to the recipient.

---

## The recipient did not receive the gift token. What should I do?

First, check the email address you used for the purchase. The token was sent to the email you entered. Check the spam or junk folder.

If you still cannot find it, contact support at support@mynaani.com. Include the email address you used to buy the gift and, if you have it, the order confirmation number.

---

## How does the recipient redeem a gift?

1. The recipient signs in or creates a free mynaani account.
2. They go to the gift redemption page.
3. They enter the token exactly as it was given to them.
4. They select "Redeem."
5. Access is added immediately.

If the token does not work, make sure it was entered exactly as written. Tokens are sensitive to spaces, dashes, and capital letters.

---

## Can the recipient see who bought the gift?

No. The recipient only receives the token. They cannot see the purchaser's payment information or email address.

## I made a mistake with the recipient's email. Can I change it?

The token is sent to the email address you enter at checkout. If you entered the wrong email, you may not be able to recover the token.

Contact support at support@mynaani.com as soon as possible. We can check whether the token has been used and help you move it to the correct account.

## What if the recipient already has access?

A gift token can only be used by an account that does not already have paid access. If the recipient already has Modules 2, 3, 4, and 5, the token will not work.

If you bought the gift by mistake, contact support for a refund within 30 days.

## What if the learner needs help with a lesson?

The learner can use the help page inside the curriculum for common questions. If a lesson is confusing, they can also write to support. Mynaani does not provide one-on-one tutoring, but we can explain the lesson or address a technical issue.

## What if the learner is too old to learn this?

Mynaani was built for older adults. The science behind the program shows that the barrier is rarely age. It is usually design that asks too much too quickly. Mynaani removes pressure, explains terms plainly, and lets the learner go at their own pace.

Confidence is part of the learning. Mynaani is designed to protect it.

## Can I manage or revoke access for the recipient?

A gift token gives the recipient their own account. You cannot manage their lessons or see their answers. Their account is private.

If you bought the gift through a community or facility program, the program staff may have different visibility. Contact the program for details.
