**Date:** 2026-08-27

3. Headline: "Learn AI at your own pace".
4. Subheadline: a single short sentence.
5. Single primary CTA in the card: **"How it works"**.
6. Clicking "How it works" opens the `HowItWorksDialog` (the existing explainer modal).
7. The `HowItWorksDialog` footer still offers a low-pressure onward option.
