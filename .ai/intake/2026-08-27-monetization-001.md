# Intake — MONETIZATION-001: Sustainable free vs. paid model for Mynaani

**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Requester:** Product  
**Process:** v9.51  
**Scope:** Research best-in-class, externally verifiable monetization models for older-adult digital learning, specifically whether an entirely free course is viable and what proven alternatives exist.

---

## Motivation

The free tier of 16 curriculum units may be too generous to sustain without external funding. We need evidence-based guidance on whether an entirely free model is feasible, and if not, which hybrid model (freemium, sponsorship, B2B/institutional, grants, affiliate, or paid) is best-in-class for the older-adult AI-learning app category.

## Research questions

1. Can a fully free older-adult digital-learning product be viable? Under what conditions?
2. What funding/sustainability models do best-in-class products in this space use?
3. What are successful examples at the intersection of older adults, digital skills, and AI?
4. How do geragogy, trust, and older-adult WTP constrain monetization?
5. Which model best fits Mynaani's architecture, flow, and Process v9.51 constraints?

## Boundaries

- No immediate code changes.
- No revocation of previously granted free access.
- Respect ADR 0021; any new pricing structure requires a new ADR if it supersedes.

## Success criteria

- At least 5 external, published, verifiable sources.
- Force-rank at least 5 monetization approaches.
- Evidence-based recommendation.
- Process artifacts: preflight, phase inventory, research report, ontology, KG update.
