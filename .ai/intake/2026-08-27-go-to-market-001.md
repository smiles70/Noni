# Intake — GTM-001: Day-one sales and marketing plan for Mynaani

**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Requester:** Product  
**Process:** v9.51  
**Scope:** Research and produce a single, actionable go-to-market guide covering market opportunity, marketing structure, sales structure, target company list, profitability, and next actions. Link to the payment-flow flowcharts.

---

## Motivation

Mynaani now has a working free/paid product (ADR 0021 + 0022) and a B2B2C sponsorship design. Before implementation, we need a grounded, verifiable go-to-market plan that answers: who to sell to, how to reach them, what to charge, what the unit economics look like, and what to do first.

## Research questions

1. What is the addressable market for older-adult AI literacy in the U.S.?
2. Who are the highest-fit B2C and B2B2C buyers?
3. What are the best channels for reaching older adults and their caregivers?
4. What is the right sales motion for B2B2C sponsors?
5. What are the first 20 organizations to contact, with evidence?
6. What does a profitability model look like under B2C + B2B2C assumptions?
7. What are the 30-60-90 day next actions?

## Success criteria

- One master HTML guide saved to local Downloads/noni.
- All claims cite verifiable published sources.
- First 20 target companies are real, named, and fit at least one sponsor profile.
- Includes Mermaid flowcharts for go-to-market and B2B2C sales.
- Process artifacts: preflight, research report, ontology, KG update.
