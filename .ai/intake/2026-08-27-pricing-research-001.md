# Intake — PRICING-001: Research and select a Mynaani pricing model

**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Requester:** Product / user  
**Process:** v9.51  
**Scope:** Deep, evidence-backed research into pricing models for older-adult digital learning, with force-ranking and a recommended fit for Mynaani's audience, website flow, and auth experience.

---

## Motivation

Mynaani must become financially sustainable, but its target audience (adults 55+) is documented as price-sensitive, vulnerable to subscription dark patterns, and likely to share accounts or receive access as a gift from adult children/caregivers. The pricing model is therefore a product, trust, and geragogy decision, not only a revenue decision.

## Research questions

1. What does published, verifiable research say about older adults' willingness to pay for digital learning / support services?
2. What pricing models do comparable successful products use?
3. Which published thought-leader frameworks (ProfitWell, OpenView, Kyle Poyar, etc.) apply to low-price B2C / prosumer edtech?
4. How do geragogy principles constrain pricing mechanics?
5. Which model best fits Mynaani's landing flow, free-tier progression, and Magic.link auth?

## Boundaries

- No code changes in this pass.
- No pricing page redesign in this pass.
- No market survey of Mynaani users (secondary research only).
- Respect existing ADR 0021; this research informs a possible amendment, not an override.

## Success criteria

- At least 5 external, published sources cited.
- A force-ranked comparison of at least 5 pricing approaches.
- A justified recommendation tied to Mynaani's persona, journey, and auth flow.
- Process artifacts: preflight, phase inventory, knowledge-graph update.
