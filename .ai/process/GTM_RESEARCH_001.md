## Executive summary

Mynaani's go-to-market should be a **dual-channel model**: low-friction B2C paid conversion ($39 / $59) for self-purchasers and caregivers, plus a **B2B2C institutional sales** motion for employers, Medicare Advantage plans, senior living operators, and corporate wellness programs. The B2B2C channel is required because AARP 2025 shows 60% of older adults are unwilling to pay for currently free digital services.

## 1. Market opportunity

| Source | Finding | Implication |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| U.S. Census, 2025 | 61.2M Americans 65+; 18% of population | Large, growing addressable market |
| AARP 2025 | 91% smartphone ownership; 90% online | Digital distribution is viable |
| AARP 2025 | GenAI use among 50+ doubled to 18% | AI literacy demand is growing |
| AARP 2025 | 60% unwilling to pay for currently free digital services | B2C is capped; B2B2C needed |
| AARP/NAC 2025 | 63M unpaid family caregivers; 29% sandwich generation | Gift/caregiver channel is real |
| Stanford GSB / GetSetUp | 80% government, 20% healthcare payer mix | Institutional payers are proven |
| Forbes/AARP | 50+ drives more than half of consumer spending | Money exists, but value must be clear |

## 2. Marketing channels

- **YouTube** long-form explainers — 88% of 55+ use weekly.
- **Facebook + Facebook Groups** — highest 50+ social usage; caregiver groups.
- **Google Search / SEO** — older adults search before downloading.
- **Email newsletter** — trusted nurture channel.
- **Community partnerships** — libraries, senior centers, AARP chapters.
- **B2B2C outbound** — LinkedIn/email to wellness, member-experience, and HR leads.

## 3. Sales motion

- **B2C:** product-led; optimize paywall, refund, trust.
- **B2B2C:** consultative sales with per-seat pricing ($25-30 blended).

## 5. Profitability

- Year 1 illustrative: 5,000 B2C sales at $39 avg + 10 orgs x 200 seats at $25 = $245,000 revenue.
- Costs: Claude API, hosting, support estimated at ~$45,000.
- Gross profit before marketing/salaries: ~$200,000.

## 6. 30-60-90 day actions

1. First 30 days: publish content, run $500 ad test, build B2B one-pager.
2. 30-60 days: analyze ads, start email nurture, send 20 personalized B2B outreaches.
3. 60-90 days: close first B2B2C pilot, double winning channel, collect testimonials.
