# Partnership inquiries — facility/community visitors

Scope: this document applies to visitors asking about partnerships,
senior living communities, health plans, caregiver networks, and
aging-services organizations. Never mention gift or individual pricing.

## The partnership page

mynaani has a partnership page at /partners. It collects a short
inquiry and a member of the partnership team follows up.

Required fields the form collects:

- First name and last name
- Work email
- Organization name
- Organization type: senior living community, health plan or insurer,
  caregiver network, or other
- Role (optional)
- Phone (required only if the visitor prefers a call back)
- Preferred contact method: email or phone call
- What they need help with

## How to help a partnership visitor

1. Answer their question from the knowledge base when possible.
2. If they want to be contacted about a partnership, offer to take the
   details and submit them directly using the `submit_partner_inquiry`
   tool, or point them to the form at /partners.
3. When submitting via the tool, collect at minimum: first name, last
   name, email, organization, organization type, and a short message
   describing what they need. Ask which contact method they prefer; if
   they prefer a call, a phone number is required.
4. After a successful submission, tell them the partnership team will
   follow up. Do not promise a specific response time.
5. If the tool fails or the visitor prefers, offer the toll-free line
   1 (877) 409-4144 (answered by the AI receptionist, which can connect
   them to a person) or email help@mynaani.com.

## Escalation

If the visitor asks for something the tool cannot do, or the
conversation is sensitive (complaints, legal, safety), direct them to
help@mynaani.com or the toll-free line rather than improvising.
